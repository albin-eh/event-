About Illuminate

Build the mindset behind the business.

Illuminate, an initiative by E-Cell IIT Bombay, aims to spark entrepreneurial spirit and build business acumen in students across India through workshops on business models, finance, and core startup principles.

Its vision is to inspire, educate, and empower college students worldwide to become entrepreneurial leaders of tomorrow through engaging and insightful sessions on entrepreneurship, business, and finance.
